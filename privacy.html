SimpliLoad Privacy Statement

Version 1.0 — Hofstetter Computer Consulting LLC

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Statement describes how SimpliLoad (“Software”), developed and owned by Hofstetter Computer Consulting LLC (“Licensor”), handles data in connection with your use of the Software. It applies to customers who download, install, and operate the Software within their own environments.

2. Data Processed by SimpliLoad

The Software operates entirely within your environment and interacts with your IBM Cognos Analytics system and related infrastructure. The Software may process:

  • report definitions and metadata from your Cognos environment;
  • execution parameters and configuration values you provide;
  • performance metrics and timing information generated during load tests;
  • logs and diagnostic information stored locally within your environment.

All such data is processed locally and remains under your control.

3. Data Collected by Hofstetter Computer Consulting LLC

Licensor does not collect, store, or transmit any data processed by the Software during normal operation. The Software does not send telemetry, analytics, usage statistics, or customer data to Licensor or to any third party.

Licensor may receive limited information directly from you if you choose to provide it in the course of seeking support, such as:

  • contact details (e.g., name, email address);
  • support case descriptions;
  • optional logs or screenshots you voluntarily share.

4. Use of Customer-Provided Information

Any information you voluntarily provide to Licensor for support purposes is used solely to:

  • diagnose and resolve technical issues;
  • communicate with you regarding your support request;
  • improve the Software based on aggregated, non-identifying insights.

Licensor does not use customer-provided information for marketing, profiling, or resale.

5. Data Sharing

Licensor does not sell, rent, or trade customer data. Licensor does not share customer data with third parties except:

  • when required by law, regulation, or legal process; or
  • with your explicit consent for a specific purpose.

IBM does not receive customer data through the Software. Any data exchanged between you and IBM is governed by your agreements with IBM.

6. Security Responsibilities

The Software runs within your environment. You are responsible for:

  • securing your servers, networks, and Cognos Analytics environment;
  • controlling access to the Software and related infrastructure;
  • configuring authentication, authorization, and encryption in accordance with your policies.

Licensor takes reasonable measures to protect any support-related information you provide, but does not control or secure your internal systems.

7. Data Retention

Licensor does not retain data processed by the Software in your environment. Any information you provide for support purposes is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • resolve the support request; and
  • comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

Licensor may delete support-related information once a case is closed, subject to any legal retention requirements.

8. Customer Rights

With respect to any support-related information you provide to Licensor, you may:

  • request access to the information Licensor holds about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of support-related information, subject to legal obligations.

Licensor will make reasonable efforts to honor such requests in a timely manner.
