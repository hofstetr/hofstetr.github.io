Schedule Control for
IBM Cognos Analytics

Discover schedules. Freeze with confidence. Restore exact states when maintenance finishes.

Overview

SimpliFreeze gives IBM Cognos Analytics administrators centralized control over scheduled jobs and tasks across environments.

Instead of manually disabling and re-enabling schedules one by one during maintenance windows, migrations, or upgrades, teams can group, freeze, thaw, export, and import schedule states predictably.

Key Capabilities

  • Schedule Discovery & Organization

    Locate schedules across IBM Cognos Analytics content and group them logically for routine or change-window operations.

  • Custom Tagging

    Tag schedules by maintenance window, business unit, or workflow so the right set is always easy to target.

  • Safe Freeze & Thaw

    Pause active schedules ahead of maintenance or troubleshooting and restore prior schedule states cleanly afterward.

  • Export & Import Workflows

    Export schedule configurations for backup or environment handoffs and import them when moving between environments.

  • Private & On-Premises Control

    Runs within your own infrastructure or private cloud VM with no external telemetry or third-party data sharing.

  • Built-In Guidance

    Includes in-app help documentation to support administrators during day-to-day operations and change windows.

Compatibility

Requires IBM Cognos Analytics version 12.2.0 or later.

Verify your download

Download the SHA256 checksum alongside the trial bundle, then verify the ZIP before extracting it:

  • Windows (PowerShell): Run Get-FileHash .\simplifreeze.zip -Algorithm SHA256 and compare the result with the downloaded checksum file.
  • Linux / macOS: Keep both files in the same directory and run sha256sum -c simplifreeze.zip.sha256.
