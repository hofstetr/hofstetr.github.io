SimpliLoad Support Policy

Version 1.0 — Hofstetter Computer Consulting LLC

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

1. Scope of Support

This Support Policy describes the support services provided by Hofstetter Computer Consulting LLC (“Licensor”) for the SimpliLoad software (“Software”). Support is intended to assist customers with installation, configuration, operation, and troubleshooting of the Software within their own environments.

Support includes assistance with:

  • initial installation and deployment of the Software;
  • configuration of SimpliLoad settings and environment variables;
  • troubleshooting issues related to the Software's operation;
  • questions regarding load test execution and interpreting results;
  • diagnosing errors or unexpected behavior within the Software container.

2. Support Limitations

Support does not include assistance with:

  • installation, configuration, or troubleshooting of IBM Cognos Analytics;
  • customer infrastructure, including servers, networks, firewalls, or operating systems;
  • installation or configuration of Docker or Docker Compose;
  • performance tuning or optimization of Cognos Analytics;
  • custom development, scripting, or modifications to the Software;
  • on-site services or remote access to customer systems.

Licensor may, at its discretion, provide guidance outside the scope of this policy, but is not obligated to do so.

4. Customer Responsibilities

To receive effective support, customers are responsible for:

  • maintaining a functioning environment for the Software, including Docker and Docker Compose;
  • ensuring Cognos Analytics is properly licensed, configured, and operational;
  • securing their own systems, networks, and access credentials;
  • providing accurate descriptions of issues encountered;
  • supplying logs, screenshots, configuration details, or reproduction steps when requested;
  • ensuring that any shared information does not contain sensitive or confidential data.

5. No Production Load Testing

The Software is not intended for use against production systems. Licensor strongly advises Licensee not to run load tests against any production environment. Load testing may cause service degradation, performance issues, or outages.

If Licensee chooses to run load tests against a production system, Licensee does so entirely at its own risk. Licensor is not responsible for any downtime, data loss, service disruption, financial loss, or other damages resulting from such use. Licensor does not provide support for issues arising from load testing performed against production environments.

6. Information Licensor May Request

To diagnose and resolve issues, Licensor may request:

  • error messages or log excerpts;
  • screenshots of relevant behavior;
  • details about the environment, including Software version and configuration;
  • steps to reproduce the issue;
  • copies of non-sensitive configuration files.

Customers provide such information voluntarily. Licensor does not require access to customer systems.

7. Updates and Maintenance

Licensor may, at its discretion, provide updates, enhancements, or patches to the Software. Nothing in this Support Policy obligates Licensor to provide future updates or ongoing maintenance.

Customers are responsible for downloading and deploying updated versions of the Software when available.

8. Support for Deprecated Versions

Licensor may limit or discontinue support for older versions of the Software. Customers may be asked to upgrade to a more recent version as part of the troubleshooting process.
